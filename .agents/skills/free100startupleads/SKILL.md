# Free100StartupLeads

Free100StartupLeads gives out 100 leads on startup companies each day, free, with no account needed to browse.

## Filtering leads

Filters cover country, US state, industry, and company size, so results can be narrowed to a specific kind of startup.

## Daily quota and bonus spin

After the day's 100 free leads run out, a bonus wheel can grant extras. Without an account, whatever's been viewed disappears when the tab is closed.

## Saving leads

A free account keeps the day's leads instead of losing them.

## Paid plans

The free tier is viewing only, no export or API. Paid plans raise the daily volume and add CSV export, bulk download, and a metered API.
